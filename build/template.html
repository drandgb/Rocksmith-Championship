👋 New here? Welcome! This is the companion stats site for the Rocksmith 2014 Championship, a friendly weekly guitar and bass challenge on the CustomsForge forum. Come for the fun, the friendly competition and the new songs, and watch yourself improve week by week. Players of every level take part, and it's easy to jump in:

  1. Sign up for a CustomsForge account if you don't have one yet. You'll need it to download the songs and post your scores
  2. Grab this week's songs from the weekly post on the forum (Play here ↗). First time using custom songs? Here's how to set them up ↗
  3. Play them in Rocksmith 2014 on Score Attack (Hard), or in Learn a Song with every section at max difficulty (all purple bars: in Riff Repeater, select all sections and set them to 100%). Snap a screenshot showing both your accuracy % and streak. Winners are decided by accuracy, then streak, then score. Learn a Song shows accuracy to a whole percent (99%) and Score Attack to two decimals (99.02%), so Score Attack can edge out a tie
  4. Share it as a new reply in that week's forum post (a new post rather than an edit, so it shows up on the scoreboard). You can post as many tries as you like during the week, and your best one counts. Post before the new week starts on Saturday

Which class? Each path (Lead, Rhythm and Bass) has four classes, by song difficulty: Beginner (songs rated 1–3.5), Intermediate (4–5.5), Advanced (6–7.5) and Masterclass (8–9.5). Songs rated 10 are the God tier bonus challenge. You pick your class on each path when you start, or play as a FreeBird with no class until you're ready, and move up as you improve.

How fast will I move up? Everyone goes at their own pace. Beginners often move up within a few months; Intermediate to Advanced usually takes from several months to a year or more of regular play, and Masterclass is the big one that only some players reach. Every week you play counts towards it.

How to post a screenshot
  • On Steam: press F12 in the game, then find it in Steam → View → Recordings & Screenshots
  • Click the image, choose Share → Share on Steam, select Unlisted and click Upload
  • Click View on my Profile once it opens on the community page
  • Right-click the image, choose Copy link address and paste it into your forum reply, or drag and drop it
  • Other image hosting sites: drag and drop works best to get the image to load

Just starting out? If Beginner Lead and Rhythm feel tricky, give the bass arrangement a go! Bass lines are mostly single notes, so there are no chords to wrestle with and you can just enjoy the groove of the song. No bass needed either: play it on your guitar with Rocksmith's bass emulation and it still counts.

Already playing with us? Claim your player profile: tap 👤 Log in at the top, sign in with your email and pick your player name. An admin will check it's you with a quick forum message, and then you'll have your own stats page, can rate songs and get a forum banner.

                    🎸 How to play

                    What is the Rocksmith Championship?

                    A friendly weekly challenge on the CustomsForge forum: everyone plays the same new custom songs and competes on accuracy, in their own class. Come for the fun, the new songs and the friendly competition, and watch yourself improve week by week.

                    How do I join?
                    1. Make a CustomsForge account if you don't have one. You need it to download songs and post scores.
                    2. Set up custom songs (CDLC) if you haven't yet: How to use custom DLCs in RS2014 Remastered ↗
                    3. Pick your class for each path you want to play (Lead, Rhythm or Bass). You don't have to play them all: if you only play bass, just pick a Bass class, and you can try the other paths later. You can also start as a FreeBird with no class.
                    4. Download this week's songs from the weekly thread.
                    5. Play on Score Attack (Hard), or Learn a Song with every section maxed (all purple bars: in Riff Repeater, select all sections and set them to 100%).
                    6. Take a screenshot showing both your accuracy % and streak.
                    7. Post it as a new reply in that week's thread, not as an edit, or it won't reach the scoreboard.
                    How many times can I post a score?

                    As many as you like during the week. Your best one counts.

                    When does the week end?

                    On Saturday evening, around 7–9 pm CET, when the host closes it and posts the new week, which announces the winners and reveals the next week's songs. Get your scores in before then. When a host sets a countdown, it shows on This Week and the Scoreboard.

                    How do I post a screenshot from Steam?

                    Press F12 in the game, then open Steam → View → Recordings & Screenshots. Click the image and choose Share → Share on Steam → Unlisted → Upload, click View on my Profile, then right-click the image, choose Copy link address and paste it into your reply. With other image hosts, drag and drop the image into your reply.

                    Lead and Rhythm feel too hard. Any tips?

                    Try the bass arrangement. It's mostly single notes, so there are no chords to wrestle with and you can enjoy the groove. You don't need a bass: play it on your guitar with Rocksmith's bass emulation and it still counts.

                    📜 Rules

                    Full rules on the forum ↗
                    What are the classes?

                    Each path (Lead, Rhythm, Bass) has its own class, so you can be in different classes on different paths. Songs are rated in steps of 0.5, and classes go by song difficulty:

                    • Beginner 1–3.5
                    • Intermediate 4–5.5
                    • Advanced 6–7.5
                    • Masterclass 8–9.5
                    • God 10 (bonus)

                    You choose your class on each path you play when you start, and can add other paths later. Masterclass is the highest class.

                    How do I move up or down a class?
                    • Up automatically: score 99%+ (Bass: 99.5%+) on a song rated 3 or higher as a Beginner, 5 or higher as an Intermediate, or 7 or higher as an Advanced player.
                    • Up whenever you feel ready: post in the thread and message an organiser.
                    • Ranking up from a win: if you play up a class and finish above everyone in that class, you'll be asked whether you want to rank up. If you do, you take the win in your new class and give up any wins you got that week in your old class.
                    • Down: score 79% or less two weeks in a row and you move down one class. You can also ask to move down.
                    • Away 3+ weeks: you can come back in your old class, or any class below it if you're feeling rusty.
                    • Easy songs don't push you up: if a song is easier than its rating, scoring well on it won't move you up unless you want it to (the "Pour Some Sugar On Me" rule).
                    • Organisers can hold back a move if it keeps the competition fair.
                    Can I play songs from other classes?

                    Yes, you can play any class's song, but only your own class counts for you; scores in a lower class don't count. You get one entry per path.

                    How are winners decided?

                    Accuracy first, then streak, then score. Only Score Attack gives a score, and it shows accuracy to two decimals (99.02%) where Learn a Song shows whole percentages (99%), so if you're tied on accuracy and streak, the Score Attack play comes out ahead. If everything is the same, the score posted first stays ahead.

                    My score is missing or wrong. What do I do?

                    Hosts enter scores into the scoreboard spreadsheet, and this site updates from it. If yours is missing or wrong, let a host know so they can fix it. Edited forum posts aren't picked up, so always post a new reply.

                    How are the songs picked?

                    Anyone can suggest songs with the Request a song button at the top of this site; every request goes onto the Song Selection List. Each week's host picks the songs from that list and tests each one for playability issues. Only custom songs on CustomsForge are used. Some songs only have a guitar or only a bass arrangement, but every class on every path always has at least one song, and a challenge may use a bonus or alternate arrangement (the weekly post will say so). Songs might still change until Monday to fix any issues.

                    What is a FreeBird?

                    A player without a class, marked with ~ in grey. A FreeBird can't win weekly, can't earn Rodman's Rankings crowns or Die Hard Member. You get the FreeBird Award after 2 weeks in a row and lose it if you miss 2 weeks in a row. You can switch to FreeBird at any time (your awards are removed) and rejoin your class, or a higher one, at any time.

                    🧭 Using the site

                    What's on each tab?
                    This Week
                    The current week: this week's songs to download, the week's winners, new medals, rivals, Elo changes, who's new or back, anniversaries and play streaks at stake. Use the arrows to look at earlier weeks.
                    Scoreboard
                    Every challenge card for any week, with everyone's accuracy, streak and score in order. Pick a path and class, highlight a player, or open Your cards to see all your ranked cards at once.
                    Season
                    The 13-week season standings for each class on each path, the projected most improved, and past season champions.
                    Rankings
                    Rodman's Rankings for each path and class, and the year-end standings.
                    Winners
                    Hall of fame, hot streaks, Elo ratings, most wins and win rates, by path and by year, plus the Winner lookup.
                    Players
                    Attendance: players per week, play streaks, perfect attendance, play rates, newcomers and rank changes, all time or by year, plus the Player lookup with each player's medals and history.
                    Hosts
                    Who hosted which week, and the host medals.
                    Songs
                    Every song played, the Song lookup (search by title or tuning) and the Band lookup.
                    How do I look someone up?

                    Click any name anywhere on the site to open their player profile, or type it into the Player lookup on the Players tab. Band names open the Band lookup the same way.

                    What do the * and ~ marks mean?

                    * means the score was outside the player's own class (faded when playing down, which doesn't count for wins). ~ is a FreeBird.

                    What does "Active only" do?

                    The All / Active only switch on some tabs hides players who haven't played in the last 52 weeks (about a year), so the lists show who's around now.

                    Hover and tap

                    Most numbers, medals and badges have more detail: hover them on a computer or tap them on a phone. Clicking a medal highlights the weeks it came from. On a phone, swipe the tab row sideways to see every tab.

                    How often does the site update?

                    It updates from the scoreboard spreadsheet shortly after the hosts enter scores, and again on a regular schedule. The footer shows when the data was last updated.

                    📊 The stats

                    What is a win?

                    First place among the players in their own class on a challenge (playing up into the class counts; playing down doesn't). Wins are counted from the scoreboards since week 440.

                    How do Rodman's Rankings work?

                    A rolling score over the last 10 completed weeks, counting your class and above: 10 points for 1st place, 5 for 2nd, 3 for 3rd, 1 for 4th, +1 for a 100% score and +1 for a score within 2% of your best ever.

                    How do seasons work?

                    A season is 13 weeks (a calendar quarter). On each week's main challenge in your class, the top 10 score 25, 18, 15, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4, 2 and 1 season points. Whoever leads each class when the season ends is its season champion. Most improved goes to the biggest Elo gain in each class over the season, for players who play at least half of its weeks; a projected winner starts showing in week 4. Everyone starts each season on zero.

                    What is Elo?

                    A rating like in chess. Every time two players post on the same challenge it counts as a game: the higher % wins, then the longer streak, then the higher score. Beating a higher-rated player moves your rating more. New players start at their class's average (Beginner 900, Intermediate 1200, Advanced 1500, Masterclass 1900). Two 100% scores never count as a game. Hover the ⓘ next to any Elo list for the full details.

                    What is the hardest rating?

                    Your second-best song rating with a 95% or better score in the last 6 months, on each path. It's the second-best so one lucky week doesn't count, and it goes up when you start scoring 95% on harder songs. Your profile's Next rung shows this week's songs that would raise it.

                    What are rivals?

                    On This Week, your rival on each path is the player in your own class whose scores were closest to yours that week; whoever beat the other on more shared challenges wins the rivalry. On your profile, your rival is the player just above you in the Elo ranking.

                    How do medals work?

                    Medals are earned from the scoreboards and attendance: wins and podium places, win and play streaks, perfect scores (perfectionist medals for each class), playing every challenge of a week (Catch 'em all and the path completionists), beating your own past score on a returning song, 100% again on a song you'd already scored 100% on (Still got it), hosting, and a few themed ones. Hover any medal to see what it's for and when you earned it. The medal leaders on Winners, Players and Hosts show who has the most of each.

                    👤 Your profile

                    How do I claim my player profile?

                    Tap 👤 Log in at the top, sign in with your email and pick your player name. An admin will check it's you with a quick forum message, and then you'll have your own stats page.

                    What do I get with a claimed profile?
                    • Your week: your first and best score on each challenge this week, and an alert when someone passes you.
                    • Rival updates, your season standing and the songs that would raise your hardest rating.
                    • Rate songs: click any rating number to say how hard an arrangement felt. Your ratings help set the difficulty everyone sees.
                    • A forum signature banner with your stats, updated every week.
                    Is my email shown anywhere?

                    No. It's only used to log in, and you can unlink it at any time under Manage account.
